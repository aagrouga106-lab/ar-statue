Head of a Bearded Man

A marble portrait head depicting a mature male figure with a beard.

This Roman-era sculpture shows naturalistic facial detailing typical of veristic portraiture from North Africa.

Discovered at Jebel F'kirine (Cap Bon, Tunisia), it reflects the artistic heritage of Africa Proconsularis.

Date: 3rd century AD
